Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Booking Your Desire Resort Stay

What is the Stay & Play Club and can I still use it if I book through you?+
Absolutely — you still receive every Stay & Play Club benefit when you book through us. Booking via our affiliate link does not remove you from the program. You'll still log in to the Stay & Play Club and earn all the perks. We simply expand on what the club offers by adding rate protection, personal support, and a free bikini on top.
Does the Stay & Play Club really offer the “best rate”?+
The hotel's “best rate” reflects their member price once you log into their site. It's not a full market search, and it doesn't automatically compare against outside travel platforms or unpublished promotions. It's essentially two prices: public and loyalty member — standard across most major hotel programs. What we do is different: we actively search competing platforms, limited-time promotions, private rates, and unadvertised offers. If a better price exists anywhere, we find it — usually saving hundreds of dollars off each stay.
How does the price-match process work?+
When we find a lower rate elsewhere, we handle the documentation and the formal price-match process directly with the resort — so you don't have to do anything. That's where the real savings happen. You just tell us your dates, resort preference, and room type, and we do the rest.
Am I still booking directly with the resort?+
Yes. When you reserve through us, you're still booking directly with Desire or Temptation via our affiliate link. The reservation is between you and the resort. We simply serve as your advocate, rate-finder, and concierge throughout the process — from booking through checkout.
What does booking through Room 77 cost me?+
Nothing extra. You pay the same rate you'd pay booking direct — or less, after our rate protection. On top of that, your booking supports Room 77 Life through our affiliate relationship, and you receive a complimentary Bikini Addiction bikini. Same resort. Stronger advocacy. No added cost.
What Stay & Play Club benefits do I keep?+
All of them. When you book through our affiliate link, you retain the full Stay & Play Club membership and its benefits: early check-in & late check-out (subject to availability), accumulation of Stay & Play points, discounts on select premium wine & liquor bottles, spa services, the jewelry shop and the Mystique Boutique — and more.
