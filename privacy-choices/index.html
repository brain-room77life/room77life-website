Your Privacy Choices

Last updated September 3, 2026 · Applies to visitors of room77life.com

✓ Opt-Out Preference Signal Honored — your browser sent a Global Privacy Control signal, and we have automatically set your preferences to opt out.

This page lets you control how room77life.com (“Room 77 Life”, operated by LRC 77 LLC) uses cookies and similar technologies on your device, and lets you exercise your privacy rights, including the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information under the laws of California (CCPA/CPRA), Colorado (CPA), Connecticut (CTDPA), Texas (TDPSA), Utah (UCPA), and other applicable state privacy laws.

Room 77 Life does not sell your personal information for money. Some disclosures to advertising and analytics partners may qualify as a “sale” or “sharing” under state privacy laws, which is why we provide the controls below.

Cookie preferences

Choose which categories of cookies and similar technologies this site may use on your device. Your choice is stored on this device and applies to future visits. Accepting and rejecting take the same single click.

Essential

Required for the site to function — security, load balancing, and remembering this consent choice. These cannot be turned off.

Statistics

Help us understand how the site is used so we can improve it, using aggregated usage data.

Marketing

Used to measure campaigns and show relevant offers, including tools like Meta Pixel.

What this site currently sets: the redesigned room77life.com is a static website and does not currently set statistics or marketing cookies. Essential items are limited to standard web-server functionality and this consent record. Embedded content (such as YouTube videos) is loaded in privacy-enhanced mode, which does not set advertising cookies unless you play the video. If we add analytics, a store, or marketing tools in the future, they will respect the choice you make above.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

You may request that we exclude your personal information from any disclosure that qualifies as a sale or sharing, limit the use of sensitive personal information, or exercise your other privacy rights. Fill in the form and submit — it opens a pre-filled email to our privacy contact. We may need to verify your identity before completing the request. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Automatic opt-out signals (Global Privacy Control)

We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal as a valid opt-out of the sale and sharing of personal information, as required in California, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, and other states with universal opt-out requirements. If your browser sends a GPC signal, this page detects it, applies the opt-out automatically, and confirms it at the top of this page. No further action is needed on your part.

Managing cookies in your browser

You can also delete or block cookies directly in your browser, or set it to warn you before cookies are placed. See your browser’s Help section, or visit allaboutcookies.org for instructions. Note that blocking all cookies may prevent parts of the site (such as a future store checkout) from working properly.

Additional third-party controls: Meta ad settings · Google Analytics opt-out · Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out

Your rights over your personal data

  • You may submit a request for access to the data we process about you;
  • You may object to the processing;
  • You may request an overview, in a commonly used format, of the data we process about you;
  • You may request correction or deletion of the data if it is incorrect or not or no longer relevant, or ask to restrict the processing of the data.

For more about how we handle personal data, see our Privacy Policy. If you believe we have not resolved your concern, you may also contact your state attorney general or, in California, the California Privacy Protection Agency.
