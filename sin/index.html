Event FAQs.

A few things to know about our SIN takeover on Isla Mujeres.

Have another question? Contact us.

How many couples attend the takeover?+−

The takeover will end up hosting between 150 to 200 people between the main hotel and overflow hotel. Intimate. Not overwhelming but a great number to make new friends. For reference, Desire Pearl Resort has around 196 people at 100% capacity.

Are threesomes / singles allowed?+−

You may come as a threesome! We must approve (for the safety of the guests) all threesomes before booking is final. Singles may also be considered, however you can not book as one. Email us if you are interested in either.

What is the name of the hotel for the takeover?+−

For the privacy of the hotel and our guests and their safety, the name of the hotel is disclosed only to guests upon booking confirmation.

Is food and alcohol included?+−

Yes — this is an all-inclusive event. Food, premium liquor (vodka, tequila, rum, and whiskey), and late-night snacks are all included. Event Passes are all-inclusive too — every party, theme night, and daytime event, all-inclusive except your room.

What is the average age of attendees?+−

We do not collect ages of the guests, however, most guests appear to be our age, 40s to 50 years old. There will be some guests in their 30s.

Is the takeover recommended for newbies or first-timers?+−

Absolutely! We always have newbies or first-timers at our events. We enjoy creating a safe space for all levels of experience. Even couples who aren't participating in the lifestyle, but love the sexy vibe, come back to our events. It takes all couples at different places on their journey to complete the unique vibe at Room 77.

What time can we arrive on the first day?+−

The hotel officially becomes clothing-optional at 3pm on the first day. You may arrive before that to check in. Your bags will be stored until the rooms are ready. Lunch is included on the first day.

What is the vibe like?+−

The vibe is very friendly and inclusive. Everyone who is going to the party usually starts in a group chat beginning 3 months before the event.
