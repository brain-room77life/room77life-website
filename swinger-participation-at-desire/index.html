Lifestyle Q&A – lifestyle participation

Will you be the only outcast if you don't participate in the lifestyle at Desire? The honest answer, and why everyone feels at home.

We get this question more often than one might think. It’s the whole lunch table mindset, “Will I be the only outcast?” Rest assured, if you not participate in the swinger lifestyle you will feel right at home.  On any given day Desire Pearl and Desire Riviera Maya will vary between a 60/40 and 40/60 split  for lifestyle and non-lifestyle guests. The hidden gem is that the guests are all there for a sexy time, whatever that means to you and your partner!  You may be asked if you are in the lifestyle but never judged either way.  Everyone is there to have a good time, most leave after making new lifelong friends.

Whether you are in the lifestyle or not, you’re sure to return because the fun and sexy vibe is hard to beat.
