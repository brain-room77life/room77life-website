Common questions

Good to know.

Everything about booking Desire or Temptation through Room 77 — the rate, the club perks, and what it costs you (nothing extra).

What is the Stay & Play Club — and can I still use it if I book through you?

Absolutely — you still receive every Stay & Play Club benefit when you book through us. Booking through our affiliate keeps all your club perks intact, and we add real rate protection, personal on-the-ground support, and a complimentary bikini on top.

Does the Stay & Play Club really offer the “best rate”?

The club’s rate reflects their loyalty club pricing — it doesn’t run a full market search or compare against outside platforms. We do: we actively search competing platforms, promotions, private rates, and unadvertised offers, typically saving you hundreds per stay.

How does the price-match process work?

When we find a lower rate elsewhere, we handle the documentation and the formal price-match process directly with the resort — so you don’t have to do anything. You just give us your dates, resort preference, and room type.

Am I still booking directly with the resort?

Yes. When you reserve through us, you’re still booking directly with Desire or Temptation via our affiliate. We’re simply your advocate, rate-finder, and concierge from booking through checkout.

What does booking through Room 77 cost me?

Nothing extra. You pay the same rate you’d pay booking direct — or less, after our rate protection. Your booking supports Room 77 through our affiliate relationship, and includes a complimentary bikini.

What Stay & Play Club benefits do I keep?

All of them — early check-in / late checkout, point accumulation, wine & liquor discounts, spa discounts, and jewelry & boutique discounts — plus Room 77’s free bikini and personal support.

I’m a Premier Member — can we book our trip through you?

Not on your Premier membership — those bookings run through a separate system we don’t have access to. But if you’d like to book a stay without using your membership contract or free nights, we’re happy to help and can offer all the perks listed above.
